How your audience should use it

The tool is designed for repeat use: choose ingredients, learn the gaps, add one or two foods, and save a simple rotation.

Protein first

Pick one protein anchor for every main meal. Vegetarian plates may need combining dal, dairy, tofu, soya, seeds, and grains.

Then fiber

Legumes, vegetables, fruits, seeds, and whole grains make meals more satisfying and usually improve micronutrient coverage too.

Then vitamins

Use the gap list to add natural foods like citrus, leafy greens, carrots, curd, eggs, fish, nuts, seeds, and colorful vegetables.

Nutrition values are practical estimates for education and meal planning. Requirements vary by health status, pregnancy, medical conditions, labs, medication, and clinician guidance.
